      How each feed is analyzed
      1

      Pixel check. We decode the frames. A black or frozen feed means the camera is lost.

      2

      NVIDIA Cosmos3-Reason watches the clip and reports whether the road is passable, how far you can see, and what hazards it spots.

      3

      YOLO11 counts vehicles and people per frame.

      4

      Predict and route. Lost corners spread downwind using live Open-Meteo wind. We avoid them and score each prediction at the next step.

      The clips are archived VAST footage assigned to these corners for the drill. They are not live feeds and not a verified match to these streets.
